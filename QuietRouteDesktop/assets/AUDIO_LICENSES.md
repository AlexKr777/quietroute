## App-only recorded ambience

The rain recording below is bundled only with `QuietRouteDesktop`. It is
published under CC0 1.0 Universal and was retrieved from Freesound on
2026-07-21. The app uses the public high-quality MP3 preview so the original
login-gated WAV is not redistributed.

| `audio/gentle-outdoor-rain.mp3` | Rain Ambience - Nature Sounds / JonCon_Library | https://freesound.org/people/JonCon_Library/sounds/668061/ | https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/668/668061_9580304-hq.mp3 | `c3d8de631694b4304a5c2c70e834cba6ea9e8da68899dddf47ad8c6ee687b26c` |

The Freesound source and its public high-quality preview contain ten minutes of
stereo rain. The recording contains far fewer short surface-hit transients than
the previous open-window recording. In the app it is level-matched, gently
low-passed, and played with a twelve-second equal-power crossfade so it remains
a soft outdoor bed without a hard loop seam.
